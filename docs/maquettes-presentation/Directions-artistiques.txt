Icônes dessinées en vectoriel. I1 reprend l’arc, le planeur et le relief de l’écusson du menu. I2 reprend la manche à air des sites de vol. I3 associe une vue en plan à une spirale thermique.

Limited spot colors, beautiful negative space, subtle paper tooth and layered geometric silhouettes, authentic and clean. HERO TITLE exactly “Ça plane !” once, in big bold slanted cream retro lettering with a short coral underline (capital Ç with a cedilla). Only other text “CHARGEMENT” small in a quiet corner. No buttons, menu, credit, watermark, number, progress bar or extra text. Accurate unmanned RC sailplane with one long tapered main wing crossing fuselage, a distinct small tailplane and fin well aft, slender fuselage with a clear nose; no propeller, engine or manned cockpit. Safe margins. Lake vista in broad midday sun. From a ridge of gold alpine grass, look DOWN over a wedge of teal lake and scalloped distant peaks. **Close cream glider with coral wing tips banks on the RIGHT**, beautifully foreshortened, nose pointed into open air. Upper left quiet deep teal sky for the title; bottom left loading. Big elegant oblique poster composition.
